# Matches played

Matches a club played in a season.

**Kind:** observed. A count of the club's matches in the source results.

**Formula:** count of the club's matches, home and away.

**Inputs:** match results from engsoccerdata and football-data, aggregated in
`mart__club_season`.

**available_from:** 1992-93.

**Limitations:** varies by era and by season stage. Complete seasons hold 42
matches for the 22-club years 1992/93 to 1994/95 and 38 from 1995/96. The current
season is in progress and holds fewer. This is why rate metrics such as points
per game are the fair way to compare across seasons.
