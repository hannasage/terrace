# Goals against

Goals a club conceded across its season.

**Kind:** observed. A sum of goals recorded in the source match results.

**Formula:** sum of the opponents' goals over the club's matches, home and away.

**Inputs:** match results from engsoccerdata and football-data, aggregated in
`mart__club_season`.

**available_from:** 1992-93.

**Limitations:** the current season's total is partial.
