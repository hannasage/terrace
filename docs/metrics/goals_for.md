# Goals for

Goals a club scored across its season.

**Kind:** observed. A sum of goals recorded in the source match results.

**Formula:** sum of the club's goals over its matches, home and away.

**Inputs:** match results from engsoccerdata and football-data, aggregated in
`mart__club_season`.

**available_from:** 1992-93.

**Limitations:** the current season's total is partial. Own goals are attributed
to the scoring side by the source, as in standard results data.
